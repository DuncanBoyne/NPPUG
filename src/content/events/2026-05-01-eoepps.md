## About This Event

The **East of England Power Platform Summit (EoEPPS)** was our flagship annual conference, bringing together Power Platform professionals, enthusiasts, and Microsoft MVPs from across the region and beyond.

This event is now called the East of England Summit, and the 2027 summit runs on Friday 30 April and Saturday 1 May at The Space, Norwich ([eoes.co.uk](https://www.eoes.co.uk/)).

### Event Details

- **Dates**: 1st - 2nd May 2026
- **Format**: In-person conference
