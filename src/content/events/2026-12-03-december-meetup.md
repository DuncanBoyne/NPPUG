## About This Event

Join us at **Spaces Digital Hub, Townshend House** on **Thursday 3 December** for an evening of two talks, networking and pizza. It's free, and open to everyone from your first flow to enterprise architecture.

---

## Sessions

### From Click to Install: Building a Company Store with Power Platform Code Apps

*Tammy Briggs*

From click to install, see how Tammy built a real Company Store using Power Platform Code Apps, automation and Intune. We'll follow one software request end to end and look behind the scenes at the code, architecture and decisions that bring makers, developers and IT pros together.

### Technology changes quickly. People change differently.

*Simon Greenall*

Successful technology adoption isn't about how many features we launch or how many people log in. It's about whether people understand the value, feel confident trying something new and ultimately find that it makes their working lives better.

In this session, Simon shares his experience of Microsoft adoption and explores why listening, trust, confidence, community and continuous learning can be just as important as the technology itself. Sometimes the biggest challenge with digital transformation isn't digital at all.
